# Evaluation and limitations

Current engineering evidence includes migration, structural, coverage-design, native-exchange, bounded OWL-mapping and scoped HermiT checks. The [native review](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/native-model-review.md) records official schema conformance and SDK interoperability. These do not establish independent semantic validity, complete language-rule verification, a complete OWL ontology or a deployed application.

The [HermiT report](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/hermit/report.md) records 693 passing executed cases, 281 of 295 baseline obligations exercised (95.25%) and 14 constraint obligations with explicit remaining routes. The first [SHACL batch](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/shacl/report.md) executes four scoped constraint profiles; three are PASS and C-08 remains PARTIAL. A requirement, a proposed test and an observed result remain different records. The [semantic assurance plan](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/semantic-assurance-plan.md) explains why these are not an overall semantic-correctness percentage.

Independent expert review, case-based adequacy and any database implementation are separate workstreams. Read the [evaluation record](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/README.md) and [coverage plan](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/formal-validation-plan.md).

The subsequent [endpoint suite](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/shacl/endpoints/report.md) executes all 254 ordered relation ends, with 127 paired-end integrations and direction/bound mutations. The [current SHACL coverage ledger](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/shacl/coverage-status.md) records 339 scoped PASS, one PARTIAL and 19 NOT_RUN of 359 obligations. The [catalog admission suite](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/shacl/admission/report.md) adds 77 managed metadata profiles and three explicit boundary exclusions. The only supported readiness task is CatalogInspection, not risk assessment. Full managed/conditional-profile integration, 19 constraint routes and C-08 compatibility remain pending.

The [C-23/C-24 coherence module](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/shacl/coherence/report.md) checks explicit context inclusion and revision entry/lineage/cycle conformance. It permits branching and detects recorded cycles through a transitive path. Static graph conformance does not establish historical immutability.
