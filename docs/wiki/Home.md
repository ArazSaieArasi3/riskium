# Riskium: An Ontology for Risk Knowledge

Riskium makes the subject of risk analysis distinct from the entries, scenarios, assessments and evidence describing it. This documentation explains the current conceptual model, its requirements and the limits of its evaluation.

The design snapshot is version 0.3.1. Native OntoUML validation and complete OWL formalization remain open. The planned HermiT and SHACL suites have not been executed.

Start with [scope and concepts](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/docs/overview.md), then inspect the [modeling decisions](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/docs/modeling-decisions.md), [requirements](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/requirements/ontology-requirements.md) and [evaluation](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/README.md).

The [repository](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium) owns the version-controlled model. Wiki explanations and generated references use those definitions; they do not independently redefine them.
