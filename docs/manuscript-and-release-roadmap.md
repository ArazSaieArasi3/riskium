**First major release goal:** a declared core of organizational risk knowledge works coherently for its chosen tasks, has independently reviewed critical interpretations, reproducible formal/application evidence, and a discoverable, synchronized repository/Wiki/Pages package. For a candidate v1.0.0, define the exact core and critical cohort, record actual independent judgments and resolved critical findings, test at least the claimed organizational contexts with literal oracles, reconcile the selected ontology-to-data mappings and unsupported routes, and pass exact-source publication/readback gates. Do not require an unrelated private-data migration, PostgreSQL deployment or a complete future product unless that release explicitly claims them. The number remains provisional until these gates are specified and passed.
