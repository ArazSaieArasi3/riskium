## Fixed exit rule

Publish the final release when **all MUST gates below are evidenced and no unresolved critical finding remains within its declared scope**. Every obligation has a recorded disposition: accepted with suitable evidence, explicitly out of scope, or deferred with its associated claim removed. A critical in-scope identity, false-inference, integrity or data-loss defect cannot be deferred merely to ship. Unsupported rules must use an appropriate reviewed alternative route; a HermiT NOT_RUN row must not be relabeled as a HermiT PASS because another method addresses it.

These are explicit project acceptance gates. A broadly named parent issue can stay open for later scope after its release-critical portion has a reviewed disposition. The retained claims control what must actually pass; merely narrowing the wording cannot conceal a critical defect in the retained model. Owner review must be reported as author/owner review with assistant support, with unreviewed groups visible and no independent or whole-ontology semantic-acceptance claim. Future independent review keeps its own evidence and denominator.

## What determines the point of final publication

The earliest planned decision point is **after the owner-led review packet (target rc.7, or earlier alongside technical work) and one final stabilization/freeze**. This is conditional: rc.4/rc.5/rc.6 are first bounded batches, so their release-critical residual work must also be resolved before final publication. The sequence is a work plan, not a promise that exactly five more prereleases suffice. Each failed gate gets a concrete fix and, when useful, another small rc; do not keep generating rc numbers without completing named acceptance work.

No waiting for #16 future extensions, interoperability expansion, every possible conversion or every research idea. Unclaimed additional features go to the next release series. The owner does not need to wait for a hypothetical 100% universal correctness score. No external-reviewer scheduling is required for this owner-reviewed release. A calendar forecast still depends on the remaining technical acceptance population and the owner's actual judgments; planned review is not completed review.

The existing incremental workflow intentionally publishes **prereleases only**. Final publication requires a separate implementation/review of the stable profile once these gates are accepted, with an explicit final tag, reviewed notes, immutable source, verified assets and appropriate latest designation. Removing `-rc.N` from a tag is not acceptance evidence. No stable release or final designation is authorized by a green prerelease workflow alone.
