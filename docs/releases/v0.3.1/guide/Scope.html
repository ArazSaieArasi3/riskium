Purpose and scope

Risk knowledge often mixes the subject under analysis with its records, possible scenarios, reported results and supporting sources. Riskium makes those subjects distinct so that information can be stored, interpreted and compared under declared conditions.

A RiskCase is an identifiable subject of analysis and tracking. Several entries can record the same case, and several scenarios can concern it. An entry, a scenario specification and an actual occurrence are different entities. A description of a possible event does not establish its occurrence.

The model separates an information artifact, an issued version and its expressed content. An issued payload remains historical; a later correction does not silently replace it. Assessment information needs the method, scale, dimension, basis, context and time justified for the particular task. Recorded responsibility does not establish a mandate; administrative closure does not establish risk cessation; a reported score is not automatically reproducible computation.

Riskium models risk knowledge. It does not prescribe a complete management lifecycle, establish regulatory compliance, predict losses or certify treatment effectiveness. Three heterogeneous phenomenon notions remain optional conceptual boundaries, and the local external anchor does not import an external ontology or resolve real-world identity.

The conceptual model, original requirements, mapping review and limits retain their separate authority and acceptance scopes.
