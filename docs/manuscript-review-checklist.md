# Final manuscript checks

The final manuscript review is tracked in [issue #14](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/14). Repository engineering and article review are separate tasks. [The historical 7.0.7 claim baseline](../evaluation/manuscript-review/README.md) retains its 65 rows; [approved manuscript 7.0.8](../evaluation/manuscript-review/v7.0.8/README.md) applies seven bounded amendments. [Current binary/paragraph readback](../evaluation/consistency/manuscript-readback.json) matches that approved successor. Word/PDF equivalence and every rendered page were checked; independent semantic validation and venue-specific acceptance are not implied. Reapply this checklist after a material revision.

For each scientific claim, record its manuscript location, wording, evidence artifact, exact model or dataset version, expected meaning, checked result and disposition. Tests material to the claim must be described in the method or evaluation section, with their population, oracle and limits. Engineering regression counts need not become headline scientific evidence.

## Test claims

- Distinguish structural regression, coverage-design, native schema, SDK interoperability, HermiT, SHACL, competency-question and independent semantic-review evidence.
- Link an executed-result claim to a current readable receipt. Planned obligations and intended fixtures are not executed tests.
- Describe what a pass establishes. Exchange compatibility and graph conformance do not establish domain adequacy.
- Preserve failed, unsupported, blocked and unexecuted checks when they affect the article's claims.
- Describe the scoped HermiT and SHACL profiles separately, including C-08 compatibility blocking and the external publication/identity policies. Neither suite's percentage is overall semantic correctness; cite the semantic-assurance plan and independent-review status when stating adequacy claims.
- For endpoint results, distinguish all 254 isolated cardinality obligations and 127 paired-end integrations from full managed-profile admission. Report non-applicable boundary negatives with reasons and identify supplementary path-witness mutations as test controls.

## Numbers

- Reconcile every numeric statement in the abstract, prose, tables, captions and supplementary material with the frozen source.
- State whether a count refers to the full catalog, typed core, imported anchor, selected view or complete view.
- Distinguish conceptual elements from serialization elements, coverage obligations from test cases, and executed cases from successful outcomes.
- Reconcile dataset source records, extracted scenarios, accepted case instances and query populations separately.
- Recheck the ledger after a material revision. Historical test receipts remain historical; a later model does not inherit their passes.

## Publication wording

Check references, availability links, version statements, limitations and figure/table consistency against the actual submitted package. Do not describe an unpublished Wiki or prepared Pages source as a verified public deployment. A final manuscript result requires the latest author-approved file and its own review record.

Use the post-prerelease [source/interpretation register](../references/README.md) for exact editions, inspected portions and source-access limits. Cite WATCHDOG arXiv v2 separately from its earlier DODGE v1 title. Do not promote public summaries or selected declarations into full-work, universal compatibility, compliance, feature-absence or novelty proofs. Detailed appetite/tolerance definitions remain unassessed; preserve source-qualified meanings until qualified review. The register's editorial PASS is not independent semantic acceptance.

Use the [claim-based quality synthesis](../evaluation/quality/README.md) as the source for current evidence categories and denominators, including all 25 constraint routes and the post-trace source-review overlay. Link each manuscript claim to its exact source version and population; retain unknown criticality and undefined semantic acceptance. The quality-report milestone does not audit the manuscript, grant stable scientific acceptance or turn the original trace statuses into accepted requirements. Keep any fresh software-regression count separate from historical scientific executions.
