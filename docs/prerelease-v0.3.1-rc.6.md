## Interpretation and claim boundaries

Native R-68 supportsRecordedArtifact and RX-021 pointsToEvidence retain their reviewed source/target signatures. The app envelope binds record, exact statement and evidence item; DOI/locator/origin are declared supplementary fields, not new native ontology predicates. The projection is for **SourceTraceInspection**, not complete native managed-instance admission.

The query checks native/envelope joins and source/locator equality. Separate checks bind caller-known records, native targets/types, source/locator/origin/cardinality to the inspected frozen cohort. A coherently falsified DOI or locator may still be returned by the raw query; it is **rejected** by those separate checks. Deleting the latter guard demonstrates the weakness. Matching asserted metadata does not establish source truth, independent corroboration or semantic correctness.

CI replays the committed source cohort, **not fresh PDF reading**; actual PDF inspection is a separate source-bound receipt. No private database, publisher PDF, table image, full passage or scored source table is included. Hospital PDF redistribution rights are not verified; brief original paraphrases and citation/locator/hash metadata are used. The review's supplied CC BY 2.0 attribution and changed wording are disclosed; third-party rights remain separate from repository licenses.

The original 11 organizational case admissions remain WITHHELD with no new RiskCase/RiskScenarioSpecification or occurrence assertions. Source scores/frequencies are not probabilities, and a review is not independent primary corroboration. Only supplied source editions were inspected; no exhaustive correction search, full 1023-row extraction accuracy, held-out generalization or whole-case scientific-acceptance denominator is claimed.

Native/OWL bytes, manuscript 7.0.7 and all historical CQ/HermiT/SHACL/SQLite receipts remain unchanged. The historical CQ report remains 40 synthetic executions with its original source-population status; the current scoped CQ06 supplement is separately dated. No HermiT/SHACL PASS is inferred from SPARQL. CQ07 correction lineage and CQ08's dedicated unresolved-identity population remain open under #13.
