Riskium: An Ontology for Risk Knowledge

Riskium separates the subject of risk analysis from entries, scenarios, assessments and the evidence describing it. The conceptual model owns the definitions; these explanations help readers inspect the model, its implementation choices and its limits.

The first stable bounded research artifact, v0.3.1, is published. It contains owner-reviewed research scope, not independent semantic certification or a production system. Current development also includes SQLite assessment/result profile 0.2.0 and shared-graph integration; these additions are separately versioned and are outside the original six stable assets.

Reading routes

  • Research: scope, requirements, competency questions, then evaluation.
  • Domain interpretation: overview, modeling decisions and use of the model.
  • Implementation: database assurance and DRSD, traceability and reproduction.
  • Publication: versions and surfaces, visualization, artifact identities and ordered work.
