Measurement contract

The original 674-obligation allocation and fixture oracles predate their recorded executions. This synthesis defines descriptive measures before interpreting them here; it does not retrospectively invent statistical success thresholds or claim a newly pre-registered experiment.

No weighted score, global PASS percentage, semantic-correctness probability or sum of unlike evidence units. Missing evidence stays unestablished; zero reviewed groups gives undefined acceptance, not 0% or 100% correctness. Ontology size and taxonomy shape are inventory descriptors, not semantic quality.

Quality-framework adoption decisions

  • Riskium's existing semantic-assurance plan and coverage contract: ADOPT_AS_LOCAL_EVIDENCE_PARTITION. Use the already declared formal, explicit-data, meaning, source, applicability, query, operational and reproducibility axes. Keep their distinct denominators and acceptance proposals; no universal scientific thresholds. Source: evaluation/semantic-assurance-plan.md at evidence checkpoint; evaluation/coverage-contract.json.

  • Ontology Quality Framework (OQF), R-039: NOT_ADOPTED_AS_A_FROZEN_METRIC_OR_CONFORMANCE_STANDARD. The inspected repository explicitly has no frozen quality core or approved canonical metric/threshold/weight set. It can inform future work; citing it does not certify Riskium or create an OQF adoption/conformance result. Source: https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/ontology-quality-framework/blob/90d7dfba29a6a09b1a210ae1b9ba65ebdd893c30/README.md.

  • Taxonomy-only metrics and external composite quality schemes: NOT_USED_FOR_WHOLE_MODEL_SEMANTIC_ACCEPTANCE. Counting classes, subclass links or depth cannot adjudicate case identity, source meaning, dependency, record history or intended-use adequacy. No external scoring scheme was implemented, benchmarked or silently adopted by name. Source: Local adoption decision; no uninspected external scheme is represented as implemented.

Verification, validation, evaluation, assessment and assurance are separated here by the evidence question and intended use. They are not asserted to be a universal linear lifecycle or a newly implemented external standard.

Judgment and remaining claim limits

No independent semantic adjudications, held-out task acceptance or reconciled operational evaluation are established; formal and normative gaps remain explicit. Unknown criticality is not zero critical findings. No new universal release gate or quality threshold is imposed by this report.

  • Development prerelease: published within its historical bounded profile.
  • This quality-report milestone: complete for claim/evidence synthesis.
  • Independent semantic acceptance, full normative acceptance, held-out applicability and operational preservation: not established.
  • Overall semantic-correctness percentage and number of critical semantic findings: unestimated, not zero.

The four source-review limits remain:

  • LIM-01: Full ISO/Risk IT/COBIT clause and term-definition crosswalks are unassessed; exact appetite/tolerance and risk-owner interpretations require legitimate source access and qualified review. (#7/#14).
  • LIM-02: All interpretation rows are author-side. Independent adjudication, especially vulnerability typing, case identity and responsibility grounding, remains outstanding. (#7/#11).
  • LIM-03: The selected prior-art comparison is not a systematic review, novelty proof, feature-absence proof or head-to-head benchmark. Full ROSE/WATCHDOG/attack-tree analyses and the access-blocked 2026 candidate are not assessed. (#13/#14).
  • LIM-04: The conceptual source's historical no-fundamental-contradiction statement has no reconstructed historical reading corpus here; this review supports only the explicit inspected-portion findings, not universal compatibility or compliance. (#11/#14).

Next apply this report to the latest author-approved manuscript and a location-specific claim/number ledger (#14). Formal/application work remains #4/#5/#12, independent review #7 and independently prepared case/CQ populations #13. Future extension tracking #16 does not become a validated capability. No manuscript was reviewed or modified by this synthesis.
