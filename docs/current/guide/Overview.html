Purpose and scope

Risk knowledge often mixes a subject under analysis with the records, possible scenarios, numeric results and evidence describing it. Riskium makes those distinctions explicit so that information can be stored, interpreted and compared under declared conditions.

A RiskCase is the identifiable subject of analysis and tracking. Several entries can record the same case, and several scenarios can concern it. An entry, a scenario specification and an actual occurrence are different entities. A description of a possible event is not evidence that it occurred.

The model also separates an information artifact, an issued version and its expressed content. A correction can supersede an issued version without rewriting the historical payload. Assessment results carry their method, scale, dimension, basis, context and time where required by their use.

Recorded responsibility does not by itself establish a real mandate. Administrative closure does not establish risk cessation. A reported score need not be a reproducible computation. These distinctions matter whenever incomplete or conflicting information is retained.

Scope boundaries

Riskium models risk-related knowledge, documentary history, assessment information, policy statements, responsibility information and evidence. It does not prescribe a complete management lifecycle, organizational committee structure or certification framework. It does not establish predictive accuracy, risk reduction or production readiness.

Three heterogeneous phenomenon notions remain optional boundaries rather than typed core classes. The external object anchor supplies a boundary for bearer references; external kinds retain their own identity criteria.

See the model, requirements and interpretation constraints.
