# Riskium v0.3.1-rc.4 development prerelease

## Goal and delivered behavior

Deliver the bounded C-13/C-18 alternative-route packet: application profile **0.7.0** validates selected level/scale-version/dimension joins and eligibility for direct comparison of two caller-selected assessment/result pairs. Both sides must agree on pinned method, scale, dimension, basis, context and temporal interpretation. Explicit membership and method permission must be present. Different names are unresolved without identity evidence; equal labels or numbers cannot authorize a comparison. No native conceptual definition/relation/class, OntoUML mapping or OWL axiom changes.

Exact raw/effective/shapes/result graphs, code/input/runtime hashes, caller focus and identity maps are retained.
