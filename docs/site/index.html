Riskium reference

Conceptual design 0.3.1. This generated view refers to the structured design; a native exchange has schema and SDK checks documented in the evaluation record. Complete semantic verification and OWL formalization remain pending.

Repository guide · Model source · Selected view · Requirements · Evaluation

80 catalog concepts include 77 typed core concepts and three optional boundaries. One imported anchor is recorded separately. HermiT and SHACL execution is planned.

Domains

  • Risk Phenomena: Risk-related subjects, specifications and explicitly referenced occurrences or conditions.
  • Risk Information: Managed information artifacts, their issued versions and the content they express.
  • Risk Classification: Governed vocabulary items, schemes and source-qualified classification assertions.
  • Risk Registration: Risk registers, entries, revisions, descriptions and recorded identification information.
  • Risk Assessment: Information about assessments, their results, methods, scales, bases and uncertainty.
  • Risk Treatment: Recorded treatment decisions, plans, action references and control information.
  • Risk Responsibility: Actor references and recorded responsibility or authority assignments, with optional grounded relationships.
  • Risk Workflow: Recorded analysis and handling states under identified scheme versions and temporal assignments.
  • Risk Evidence: Evidence items and qualified references recording support, challenge or contextualization.
  • Risk Monitoring: Indicator specifications, observations, reported values, thresholds and signals.
  • Risk Policy: Recorded criteria, appetite and tolerance statements and references to objectives.

Concepts

Risk Pattern Specification

A managed, reusable information specification of risk-relevant conditions and possible effects. Its identity is distinct from a particular risk case, a scenario execution and the uncertain effects themselves.

RKM-C001 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Risk Context Specification

A versioned information specification of analysis boundaries, circumstances, time horizon and objective references under which risk statements and results are interpreted.

RKM-C002 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Risk Scenario Specification

A managed information specification of a possible development of conditions, events and effects concerning one or more RiskCases. Several scenarios may concern one case; a scenario is not the case or an actual occurrence.

RKM-C003 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Risk Occurrence

An actual occurrence or change relevant to a risk scenario; a scenario or report alone does not establish that it happened.

RKM-C004 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · event

Risk Nature Assertion

A source-qualified information assertion that possible or reported effects are favorable, unfavorable or mixed relative to identified objectives and context.

RKM-C005 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Nature Term

A controlled value used to qualify the nature of effects relative to objectives; it does not classify risk independently of context. This is a managed vocabulary resource with scheme-qualified identity, not a bare string.

RKM-C006 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Risk Source

A contextual source role that an entity may play in giving rise to risk, alone or with other contributors. The catalog boundary spans possible bearer categories and is not assigned a single OntoUML stereotype here.

RKM-C007 · Risk Phenomena · OPTIONAL_PHENOMENON_BOUNDARY · untyped boundary

Predisposing Condition

A risk-relevant situation that increases susceptibility to specified occurrences or effects. Reference to it requires explicit source support and does not establish a causal mechanism.

RKM-C008 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · situation

Trigger

An actual occurrence interpreted as initiating a risk-relevant development in a specified context. A hypothetical trigger description is represented as scenario content, not as an actual Trigger individual.

RKM-C009 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · event

Consequence

An outcome relevant to objectives, considered as a referent distinct from a statement about it. Outcomes can have different ontological categories; no universal event or object stereotype is asserted.

RKM-C010 · Risk Phenomena · OPTIONAL_PHENOMENON_BOUNDARY · untyped boundary

Vulnerability

An intrinsic susceptibility of a bearer to specified risk-relevant conditions or effects. The proposed mode interpretation requires a supported bearer; security-specific exploitation meanings remain source-qualified.

RKM-C011 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · mode

Exposure

A time-qualified situation relating an affected bearer to a risk source or relevant condition. An exposure score or its description is information about that situation.

RKM-C012 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · situation

Risk State

A temporally qualified state of a risk-relevant situation, distinct from the workflow status of its record.

RKM-C013 · Risk Phenomena · FORMAL_CORE · situation

Information Artifact

A managed documentary object with an instituted identity that persists across its descriptions and issued versions. Artifact identity is distinct from a file path, a storage row and semantic content.

RKM-C014 · Risk Information · FORMAL_CORE · kind

Artifact Version

An identifiable issued documentary version of a managed information artifact. Its issued semantic content and effective dependency versions are immutable; revisions receive distinct version identities.

RKM-C015 · Risk Information · FORMAL_CORE · kind

Information Content

The abstract informational meaning expressed by artifacts or versions. Content identity requires an explicit equivalence policy and is not inferred from equal strings or file hashes.

RKM-C016 · Risk Information · FORMAL_CORE · abstract

Vocabulary Concept

A managed terminological information artifact that identifies a vocabulary meaning independently of its labels. It is a vocabulary resource, not automatically a domain universal or the real-world entity denoted.

RKM-C074 · Risk Policy · FORMAL_CORE · subkind

Risk Case

An instituted, identifiable subject of enterprise-risk analysis and tracking. It persists across its descriptions, reassessments, responsibilities and administrative record states under a governed identity policy. It is neither its register entry nor a scenario specification nor an actual occurrence.

Interpretation constraints

Risk Case is not ISO Risk

Case identity is preserved across descriptions, scores, owners and administrative states. Equal labels do not establish case identity.

C-01

No realization from description

A scenario, consequence statement, signal, plan or report alone must not create actual occurrences, outcomes, controls or effectiveness assertions.

C-02

Issued version immutability

Issued semantic content and effective dependency versions are immutable. Correction and supersession create lineage-preserving new versions.

C-03

Assessment interpretation

Results retain method/scale version, dimension, basis, relevant context and time semantics. A score is not the risk, and estimated likelihood need not be numeric.

C-04

Temporal and administrative distinction

Recorded time, reference time and validity intervals are distinct. Workflow closure does not prove risk cessation or successful treatment.

C-05

Relator participant distinctness

An asserted ResponsibilityRelator requires at least two distinct mediated individuals. If bearer and authority resolve to one actor and no further grounding exists, retain ResponsibilityAssignmentInformation without asserting the relator.

C-06

Role-qualified ownership

ResponsibleAgentRole does not automatically mean Risk Owner. Ownership mapping requires recorded responsibility type, accountability, authority and applicable context.

C-07

Threshold anchor

At least one of thresholdForIndicator or thresholdOnScale is present. If both are present, their scale/unit interpretation is compatible. Qualitative boundaries use scale semantics rather than invented numeric units.

C-08

Description aboutness

Every published RiskDescription has at least one case, pattern or scenario target. Multiple valid targets are permitted and require explicit interpretation.

C-09

Framework-qualified tolerance

Tolerance statements retain source framework/version, original meaning and context. Permissible deviation is not automatically equivalent to readiness to bear remaining risk.

C-10

Vocabulary resource identity

Vocabulary resources retain scheme-qualified identity. Labels are representations and are not OWL classes by default.

C-11

Evidence does not endorse

EvidenceReference states an evidential role such as support, challenge or context. Linking a source does not automatically prove the target claim.

C-12

Scale and dimension compatibility

A selected level belongs to the selected scale version and dimension. Results on incompatible methods/scales are not ranked merely by equal labels or numeric codes.

C-13

Heterogeneous boundaries

RiskSource, Consequence and RiskControl remain catalog notions and optional external boundaries, excluded from native formal-core class assertions. Source, outcome and control content is represented by RiskSourceReference, ConsequenceStatement and ControlReference.

C-14

Intrinsic vulnerability bearer

Every asserted intrinsic Vulnerability has exactly one substantial-endurant bearer. A reported hypothesis alone is information, not an actual mode.

C-15

Admission profiles

Raw source content is retained in staging. Managed records require identity, subject, title and provenance. Analytical readiness additionally requires the pinned context, method, scale, basis, dimension and time required for the selected task.

C-16

Multiple assertions

Multiple classifications, descriptions, actors and evidence assertions are retained with role, context, provenance and time. Scalar export requires an explicit selection rule when several values apply.

C-17

Comparable assessments

Direct comparison requires matching pinned method, scale, dimension, basis and temporal context; a governed explicit transformation may enable a different comparison. Missing data is not zero. Reported scores do not imply a reproducible computation.

C-18

Workflow semantics

State labels are scheme/version qualified. Transition rules and authority are source-backed content. Missing rule means UNKNOWN; Closed does not imply ceased risk and Treated does not imply effectiveness.

C-19

Risk Item vocabulary

In the terminology adopted by this profile, Risk Item denotes the managed risk subject modeled as RiskCase. This context-qualified mapping does not equate RiskCase with RiskRegisterEntry, RiskScenarioSpecification, ISO risk or any global Risk Item class.

C-20

Reported and computed scores

A reported RiskScoreResult may have no derivedFrom links. A computed RiskScoreResult requires at least one linked DimensionAssessmentResult, a pinned method and adequate inputs. The score kind must be recorded.

C-21

Responsibility identity

Repeated mandates between the same participants are permitted when grounding assignment, responsibility scope or validity differs. Duplicate representations of the same grounded mandate must resolve to one relator identity; participant equality alone is not an identity key.

C-22

Context relation coherence

Every analyzedInContext link is also a hasContext link for the same RiskCase and RiskContextSpecification. The broader hasContext relation may include non-analysis context.

C-23

Revision coherence

For each EntryRevision, revisionOf and inherited versionOf identify the same RiskRegisterEntry. previousRevision is irreflexive and acyclic and, when present, relates revisions of the same entry.

C-24

Version target coherence

usesContextVersion targets a version of RiskContextSpecification; usesMethodVersion a version of AssessmentMethodSpecification; usesScaleVersion a version of AssessmentScale; usesCriteriaVersion a version of RiskCriteria. Artifact-level references, when present, agree with versionOf.

C-25

Generated from the version-controlled Riskium design. The source model owns definitions; this reference is a derived view.
