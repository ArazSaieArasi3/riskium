# Reading guide

For a research review, begin with the overview, requirements and modeling decisions. Inspect the model and its diagrams, then read the evaluation limits and reproducibility instructions.

For domain review, focus on definitions, the case/scenario/entry distinction, examples and the interpretation constraints. A familiar label does not establish semantic equivalence.

For technical use, inspect the complete model, the derived typed projection and the endpoint cardinalities. Read the formal-validation plan before interpreting these records as executable OWL axioms or SHACL constraints.
