# Riskium: organizational risk knowledge

Riskium organizes organizational risk knowledge while distinguishing a continuing RiskCase from its documentary entries, scenarios, occurrences, assessments, treatment claims and evidence. The ontology has one general domain and eleven subdomains; the pharmaceutical literature is a development case, not its defining domain.

## Package and evidence

Source v0.3.8 collects the maintained reader and public evidence for author-held manuscript 7.2.3 alongside the unchanged model and SQL components. Finite engineering results and documentary source agreement do not establish whole-model semantic accuracy, independent expert acceptance or successful deployment at every organizational scale. [The work plan](Work-Plan.md) keeps those research tasks visible.
