# Read the model through its different views

> Reader 0.4.7 accompanies the v0.3.8 source package. Model/OWL remain 0.3.1 and SQL remains 0.9.0/schema 10. The current author-held manuscript is 7.2.3; its public receipt records ten pages and 36 references. Release status is established by the separate publication record.

## Retained technical checkpoint

The following technical material retains its original 0.4.5 wording, source links and dated populations. Its references to manuscript 7.0.8 or earlier releases describe those checkpoints, not the current article or an extra validation claim. [Current artifact roles](Artifact-Versions.md), [publication](Publication.md) and [work plan](Work-Plan.md) distinguish them.

The conceptual reference, OWL reference and graph answer different questions. Use the conceptual catalog to understand meaning, the formal reference to inspect asserted commitments, and the graph to follow selected connections. None replaces the semantic source.

## Conceptual diagrams and lookup

The [diagram directory](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/reference/diagrams/index.html) links the selected and complete conceptual views. Begin with the selected diagram to follow the central distinctions; open the original SVG to zoom into labels. The complete diagram includes the full catalog and optional boundary concepts.

The [term directory](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/reference/search.html) searches concept, relation and constraint labels, identifiers and definitions. Results link to the conceptual reference and, where the term belongs to the bounded export, its OWL declaration and text neighborhood.

## WIDOCO reference

The [WIDOCO reference](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/widoco/index-en.html) retains the generated class/property descriptions and asserted axioms. Authored sections explain the export's scope, OWL interpretation, namespace declarations and evidence. Local filtering supports lookup; in-page term links remain available without JavaScript.

The original Turtle is preserved byte for byte. RDF/XML, JSON-LD and N-Triples downloads are checked for graph isomorphism. This establishes agreement of the serializations, not semantic correctness.

## WebVOWL and the text alternative

The [WebVOWL graph](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/webvowl/index.html) is deployed. It starts with a selected concept and its immediate asserted connections. Search for a class or relation to read its definition, switch to the complete projection when needed, or use fit, zoom and layout controls. The layout pauses after settling; moving the view does not edit the ontology.

The fixed projection contains 78 named classes, 127 object properties and 66 named subclass links. It represents 808 of 2,087 RDF statements; 1,279 remain outside this view, including anonymous expressions and other axioms. The [coverage inventory](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/webvowl/coverage.json) lists omissions. A neighborhood is a further visible subset of this projection. No causal, SHACL, SQL or complete OntoUML interpretation follows from its edges.

The [text explorer](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/explore/index.html) follows asserted parents, children, domains and ranges without requiring JavaScript. It provides a reading and keyboard alternative to the canvas. Neither explorer infers new facts, uploads an ontology or queries a remote conversion service.
