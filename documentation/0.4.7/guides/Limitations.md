## Semantic and implementation limits

The bounded OWL export excludes optional boundaries and their bridges and does not encode every native OntoUML rule. Native exchange/schema/SDK checks are not a full native-language or independent interpretation certificate. Historical HermiT and SHACL results retain their partial and unexecuted obligations.

Public SQL implements selected storage/native-export/subclause mappings and documentary lifecycle behavior. All 306 native IDs have recorded dispositions; **236 mappings remain deferred**, and fourteen source/scenario/evidence routes are partial. Documentary provenance, actors and contexts do not establish truth, occurrence, authorization or corroboration.

Reinstatement, broader assertions, formulas, workflow, full temporal rules, PostgreSQL/private migration and real operational acceptance remain open. The internal Python API is not a deployed HTTP service. See [database assurance](Database-Assurance.md) for implemented behavior and its limits.

## Empirical and paper limits

All **eleven organizational case admissions remain WITHHELD**. Independent semantic ratings remain **zero**. Source preservation and arithmetic fidelity do not establish independent RiskCases, calibrated prediction, generalization to held-out data or organizational benefits.

The approved manuscript **7.0.8** has been checked against its actual Word/PDF baseline. Later SQL executions remain outside its evaluated claims. The engineering release does not imply article publication, a DOI or conference acceptance. [Paper alignment](Traceability.md) identifies the retained and withdrawn claim dispositions.
