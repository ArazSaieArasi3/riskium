# Development and research priorities

**[v0.3.5](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/releases/tag/v0.3.5) is published.** Its completed scope is evidence-reference lifecycle behavior and current artifact traceability, with model 0.3.1, ORSD 0.4.3, original DRSD 0.1.0 and approved manuscript 7.0.8 unchanged. Documentation 0.4.2 maintains the explanation of that release.

## What the release added

| Completed result | What it enables |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence-reference lifecycle 0.7.0 | Explicit documentary correction, selection, withdrawal, clearing and as-of retrieval; 43 fixed controls |
| Current consistency 0.3.0 | Routes from requirements and source IDs to implementations, evidence, limits and paper dispositions |
| Selected and verified source package | 29 compatible components, six public assets and preserved prior release history |
| Shared documentation baseline | Common explanatory guides for native Wiki and Pages, alongside generated references |

These are bounded engineering results. The [evaluation guide](Evaluation.md) explains what they do and do not support.

## Documentation maintenance

The maintained view aligns the released status, reader paths and common Wiki/Pages explanation. Its editorial, rendered and actual-channel verification is recorded separately from source publication; the tracking links below lead to that evidence.

## What comes next

| Priority | Required outcome | How it will be obtained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define the next development package | One bounded problem, declared requirements, expected outcomes and acceptance limits | Review the open semantic/application work, choose scope, then commit fixed expectations before implementation |
| Strengthen research evidence | Adequate sources and supported case claims, with actual independent scrutiny | Close the relevant source/case gaps and collect real independent assessments |
| Prepare a conference submission | Venue-specific manuscript and reproducibility package with author approval | Choose venue/track, resolve retained-claim evidence, reproduce the final paper package and obtain coauthor approval |

The selected next release is **v0.3.6**, with assessment selection as its bounded scope. Publication follows completed reader review and verified source assets; no deadline is claimed. A clean documentation surface does not establish a submission-ready paper or a submission date.

## Remaining scope

All 306 native IDs are accounted for, but 236 mappings remain deferred and fourteen source/scenario/evidence routes are partial. Broader native assertion semantics, reinstatement, rules/formulas, workflow, PostgreSQL/private migration and operational acceptance remain open. All eleven organizational case admissions remain withheld, and independent semantic ratings remain zero. [Limitations](Limitations.md) and [traceability](Traceability.md) keep these boundaries visible.
