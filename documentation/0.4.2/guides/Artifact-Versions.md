Documentation **0.4.2** explains the published baseline and the separate candidate. Source publication is tracked in [#61](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/61). Riskium versions several artifacts separately. Source release **v0.3.5** packages the research and engineering files; it does not mean that the ontology or paper has that version.

## Current views and frozen history

Wiki and `/current/` are maintained reading surfaces. They point to exact semantic/evidence sources and the published release. For scholarly reproduction, use an immutable source tag or commit rather than assuming the current page will always retain the same wording.

The [version index](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/versions/index.html) exposes four frozen documentation snapshots. They preserve their original content and version labels. Earlier 0.3.0 and 0.4.0 documentation source namespaces also remain intact. A historical statement that work was pending describes its own checkpoint.

There are twelve published source releases: seven development prereleases and five stable distributions. The latest is [v0.3.5](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/releases/tag/v0.3.5); its [publication record](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/59) verifies preservation of all eleven earlier releases. [Publication](Publication.md) explains downloads and citation. The selected next distribution is **v0.3.6**, covering context-qualified assessment selection; its publication remains pending [#61](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/61).
