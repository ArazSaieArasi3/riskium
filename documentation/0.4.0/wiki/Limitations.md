# Scope and remaining limitations

Published stable source is **v0.3.4**. Current documentation **0.4.0** explains model **0.3.1**, the separately bound later engineering and target **v0.3.5**. Source agreement and successful software tests are not whole-model scientific validation.

## Semantic and implementation limits

The bounded OWL export excludes optional boundaries and their bridges and does not encode every native OntoUML rule. Native exchange/schema/SDK checks are not a full native-language or independent interpretation certificate. Historical HermiT/SHACL outcomes retain their unexecuted/partial obligations.

Public SQL has selected storage/native-export/subclause mappings and documentary lifecycle behavior. All 306 native IDs retain dispositions; 236 remain deferred and fourteen source/scenario/evidence routes are partial. Documentary provenance, actors and contexts do not establish truth, occurrence, authorization or corroboration. Reinstatement, broader assertions, formulas, workflow, full temporal rules, PostgreSQL/private migration and real operational acceptance remain open.

## Empirical and paper limits

All eleven organizational case admissions remain WITHHELD. Independent semantic ratings remain zero. Source-preservation and arithmetic fidelity do not establish independent RiskCases, calibrated prediction, generalization to held-out data or organizational benefits. The actual approved 7.0.8 paper baseline is checked; later SQL execution remains outside its evaluated claims.
