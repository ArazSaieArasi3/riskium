# Modeling decisions

## Cases, entries and scenarios

Case identity persists across descriptions, reassessments and administrative record changes. Equal text or equal scores do not automatically merge cases. A change in subject or context may require an explicit identity review.

Entries document cases. Scenario specifications describe possibilities. Occurrences refer to actual phenomena and require their own evidence. These distinctions are preserved even when one source uses a shared everyday label.

## Documentary identity

An artifact, an issued version and expressed content have distinct identity roles. Issued payloads remain immutable; corrections create a new version and an explicit replacement relationship. Record time, reference time and validity time are different.

## Results and comparison

A supplied score is retained as reported unless the calculation has justified inputs and a pinned method. Equal values do not authorize comparison across incompatible scales, dimensions, contexts or versions. Missing information remains unknown.

## Responsibility and workflow

Assignment information can exist without an asserted actual mandate. Actual participants and mandate identity require evidence. A workflow label is interpreted under its declared scheme and rules; closure alone implies neither cessation nor treatment effectiveness.

## Boundaries and dependence

Heterogeneous real-world referents are optional boundaries. A vulnerability mode depends on one justified object bearer. A recorded claim about vulnerability is not itself an actual mode.

## Current limits

The native exchange now has official schema and SDK interoperability checks, preserved source fields and explicit generalization-set decisions. See the [native review](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/evaluation/native-model-review.md) for its scope and legacy-tool limitations. A [bounded OWL mapping](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/ccc89a184a618ccc2c107ccbf27a15232060557e/evaluation/owl-mapping-review.md) and scoped HermiT/SHACL modules have executed. Complete semantic verification and independent semantic review remain open; the engineering evidence retains its documented scope.
