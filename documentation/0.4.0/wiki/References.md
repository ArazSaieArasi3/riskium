## Read the evidence at its declared strength

A documentary source link preserves provenance; it does not establish truth, endorsement, occurrence, organizational authority or independent corroboration. Public ISO/ISACA overview descriptions support only their inspected comparisons. Exact normative definitions and conformance require legitimate corresponding sources and a declared comparison population.

Related-work comparisons describe inspected contributions and unassessed documentary-version contracts. They do not assert that equivalent capabilities are absent from every prior ontology or that Riskium outperforms them. [Publication comparison](Publication-Comparison) concerns documentation routes and presentation evidence at its dated checkpoint, not scientific superiority.
