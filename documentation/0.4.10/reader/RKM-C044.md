## Current documentary comparisons

**Disposition:** `ANALOGY` · reviewed 2026-11-10.

Retain the response analogy and separate actual treatment performance from its plan. Intended risk modification does not by itself establish observed effectiveness.

A documented plan alone does not establish an executed treatment.
