# External definitions and documentary dispositions

Scientific source **0.3.2** · documentary review **0.2.0** · reader **0.4.10**. Prepared for package **v0.3.10**; not published.

Assistant-supported current-source documentary dispositions; no exhaustive search, formal alignment or independent semantic rating.

## Method and boundaries

The eight review packets preserve authored definitions, current source identities, edition/date/locator, selected paraphrases, context and explicit deferrals. Packet-specific access evidence is retained; a full-response hash, selected passage fingerprint and metadata-only access are different evidence routes. Modeling foundations remain separate from domain comparisons.

All **82** concepts have one disposition: **40 RELATED**, **20 CONTRAST**, **19 ANALOGY** and **three NEEDS_LICENSED_SOURCE**. The last three are explicit deferrals to [#78](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/78), not completed normative mappings. Independent interpretation remains [#23](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/23). No exhaustive search, absence of other relevant work, formal equivalence or superiority is asserted.

## D09 Risk Evidence

| Concept | Disposition | Comparison boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Evidence Reference](RKM-C065.md) | RELATED | Retain derivation as one related provenance pattern, not as a definition of evidential use. Evidence may be used without deriving a new entity or endorsing a claim; a citation does not establish independent corroboration. |
| [Evidence Item](RKM-C066.md) | RELATED | Retain the external comparison and separately document the role foundation. EvidenceItem inherits artifact identity; RE-001 makes participation depend on grounded use. It does not classify every artifact as evidence everywhere. |
| [Evidence Use Relator](RKM-C075.md) | ANALOGY | Use an analogy to provenance usage while preserving the different participant structure. Riskium objectifies a grounded use linking a qualified reference and an evidence artifact, with context and purpose. RE-001 and RE-002 mediate the participants; RE-003 records grounding. OntoUML C2 supplies a minimum-count rule; distinct participants are an additional Riskium C-26 commitment, not a conclusion from that sum. |
| [Evidence Reference Role](RKM-C076.md) | CONTRAST | Distinguish a function description from the documentary reference that participates in grounded use. GE-001 preserves reference identity through EvidenceReference and InformationArtifact; RE-002 supplies the role dependency. The reference can exist as a record of proposed use without this role membership. |
