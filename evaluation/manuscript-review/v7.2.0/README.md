# Author-held manuscript 7.2.0 receipt

The ten-page CSICC 2026 candidate is separately saved as Word and PDF. This folder records their exact hashes, the source/model/ORSD/archive versions and a [claim-to-evidence ledger](claim-ledger.md); it does not publish or submit the article. [The receipt](receipt.json) records the bounded new results and historical populations separately. Coauthor approval, submission and a source-release publication are separate actions.

Relative to 7.1.0, the exact article changes are three body paragraphs: the method points to the versioned receipt instead of repeating all component numbers; reproducibility distinguishes the public baseline, replayable draft branch and author-held evaluation packet; the case study explicitly states that the literature was rewritten as a qualified relational corpus and did not supply ready-made organizational RiskCases. The ontology model, bibliography, figures, tables and measured outcomes are unchanged.

The [public replay subset](../v7.1.0/supplement/README.md) provides code, original snapshots and a verifier for selected structural, pattern and SQL–SPARQL observations. The original case archive is author-held, with exact member hashes and source locators in this receipt and the historical [case evidence index](../ledger.json). A fresh checkout of the draft branch can replay the public subset; it cannot reconstruct the author-held archive without the separately preserved archive. This limitation is declared in the manuscript and ledger.

The exact Word/PDF were rendered and inspected page by page; the audit also checks all paragraph/table text in the PDF, table borders, figure preservation, 34 first-use citation positions and six requested author works.
