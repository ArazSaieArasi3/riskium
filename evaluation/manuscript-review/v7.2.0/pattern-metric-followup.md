**Article-ready bounded statement:** “On the pinned typed core, eight selected OntoUML trigger screens produced 97 candidate occurrences with six passing detector controls. Five additional catalogue rules yielded 65 further structural prompts, while seven lacked the required modeling vocabulary. None has an independent semantic defect verdict. Eleven subdomain graph observations describe the catalog design; Ensan–Du semantic cohesion and coupling require a declared modular axiom and symbol allocation and are therefore not scored.” This is an optional future article revision; it is **not inserted** into the author-held 7.2.0 binary.

The original [OntoUML anti-pattern catalogue](https://ontouml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/anti-patterns/) supplies the family descriptions. [Ensan's thesis, ch. 7, Definitions 7.3–7.10](https://www.collectionscanada.gc.ca/obj/thesescanada/vol2/002/NR89158.PDF) gives the strong/moderate local/external dependency sets and weights corresponding to Ensan and Du, *Information Systems* 38(5), 2013, DOI 10.1016/j.is.2012.11.012. Its `MLD = n(n−1)` denominator uses **local concepts and roles**, not the count of catalog classes alone. The project has not declared one independently executable ontology module per catalog subdomain or allocated relation symbols and axioms to them. A future score needs that design decision, its implementation/compatibility review, fixed weighting and a validated semantic dependency computation. Independent interpretation belongs to #23; changes to native/OWL/model semantics require their own review and regression route under #4/#5.

The report's `actual_defects_confirmed = 0` is paired with `actual_defects_status = UNKNOWN_NO_INDEPENDENT_SEMANTIC_ADJUDICATION`; it is **not** a finding that the model has no defects. The seven excluded catalogue families are excluded only from this typed-core screen; they are not certified inapplicable to any future version or to every native artifact.
