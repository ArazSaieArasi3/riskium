# Prepared independent review protocol 0.1.0

The [protocol](protocol.json) prepares the first acceptance criterion of #23. It predeclares 40 purposively selected groups from the unchanged 319-group historical inventory: 36 core identity/document/referent/responsibility/version/scale groups and all four boundary/anchor groups. Each row retains its exact source hash and all review aspects, with source-packet paths and explicit counterexample prompts. This cohort is not a representative sample and supplies no whole-ontology accuracy denominator.

No reviewer has been appointed or contacted. The [response file](responses.json) is empty: zero eligible reviewers, zero actual independent ratings and zero adjudications. The existing eleven author/owner decisions remain attributable owner review and are not copied into independent ratings. The hypothetical prompts and automated test results are not human responses.

Two distinct eligible people must submit independent initial ratings: a risk-domain reviewer and an ontology engineer. They must identify relevant expertise, affiliations/relationships and conflicts, and must not be Riskium authors/designers or this assistant. Every declared aspect needs an attributable rating, rationale and source/counterexample evidence. Missing or abstaining responses remain unresolved. Initial ratings are append-only; corrections retain lineage.

The protocol pins current source/cohort/inspection packets by hash. Initial packets contain model commitments and counterexample questions; existing author dispositions are contextual material to inspect after initial independent ratings. Preserve original ratings, then ask a third independent adjudicator to resolve disagreements with reasons. Report paired review completion and accepted/revised/rejected/unresolved results separately; agreement is not a probability of truth. A retained critical defect needs correction or explicit withdrawal of the affected claim.

`python tools/check_research_backlog.py --check` verifies source/cohort bindings and the unfilled response state. A future response-ingestion implementation must preserve actual attribution and aspect-level judgments before changing this state; it cannot create results automatically. #23 remains open for actual independent reviews and reasoned dispositions. The published owner-reviewed v0.3.1 does not depend on inventing them.

The later database profile 0.2.0 has its own engineering requirements and evidence; this protocol remains pinned to the original v0.3.1 source cohort and does not silently supply independent acceptance of the newer profile. Actual independent ratings remain zero.
