# Open research and next steps

The coordinated v0.3.9 publication selects the tested source and maintains its reader routes. The following objectives retain their own acceptance criteria; publication is not their completion.

| Priority | Next bounded research output |
|---|---|
| Source curation and alignment. | Continue [#85](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/85) and [#78](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/78), distinguishing supporting definitions, analogies, proposed mappings and independently accepted equivalences. |
| Database and pharmaceutical corpus. | Scope [#12](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/12), [#35](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/35), [#36](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/36) and [#71](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/71) into small schema, mapping, dataset-lineage and SQLite/PostgreSQL experiments with explicit source versions. |
| Semantic modules. | Define executable module boundaries and dependencies before computing the semantic COH/COP measures in [#77](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/77). |
| Independent and application evaluation. | Extend [#23](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/23) with documented independent review and [#13](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/13) with independently prepared case populations and task evaluation. |
| Interoperability and product direction. | Investigate [#72](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/72), [#73](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/73) and [#74](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/74) through service contracts and a small application architecture before claiming a production product. |

An eventual first major release should qualify a clearly defined usable scope. It need not imply every future research objective is solved. [All open issues](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues) retain the authoritative task states.
