# Competency questions and executable tasks

Competency questions describe the intended information needs: which case is recorded, what objective and context qualify it, which source supports a claim, which assessment method and scale were used, and what assignment or treatment was recorded. The [question inventory](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/tree/ce0baefa3495d1bc3b42f56ad578154d5bc4f178/requirements/cq) preserves its original formulation and evidence.

Executable query answers and shape checks cover selected tasks and fixtures. In the successor tests, a case can have zero entries, two entries can concern one case, and equal labels do not collapse distinct identities. Analytical readiness is distinguished from approval under a chosen local operational task. Missing explicit information is checked at the task layer rather than treated as a universal closed-world fact.

The bounded SQLite/RDF queries and the complete requirement population have different denominators. Passing selected query comparisons does not establish that every possible question or application is covered.
