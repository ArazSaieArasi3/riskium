# Ontology requirements

Riskium supports identifiable analytical risk items, separate documentary records and version lineage, context-qualified assessment, grounded responsibility and evidence, and locally declared operational profiles. These purposes guide the shared core rather than prescribing one organization's full management process.

The [original requirements](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/ce0baefa3495d1bc3b42f56ad578154d5bc4f178/requirements/ontology-requirements.md) and [competency-question inventory](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/tree/ce0baefa3495d1bc3b42f56ad578154d5bc4f178/requirements/cq) retain their historical source bindings. The successor's [twenty declared cases](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/ce0baefa3495d1bc3b42f56ad578154d5bc4f178/evaluation/manuscript-review/v7.2.3/issue92-impact/acceptance-oracles.json) add explicit no-approval/no-entry controls, identity/record distinctions, objective/context qualification, selector safety and preservation checks. Their executed results belong to the source qualified for v0.3.9.
