# Riskium: ecosystem and enterprise risk knowledge

Riskium is a general conceptual ontology for organizing risk knowledge across ecosystem and enterprise contexts. A **RiskCase** is an identifiable risk item for analysis and tracking relative to objectives and context; a specified perspective may qualify it as a threat or opportunity. Register entries, scenarios, occurrences, assessments and evidence describe related aspects without replacing its identity.

**Published package v0.3.9** selects scientific model **0.3.2**, explicit-data task profile **0.1.0** and reader **0.4.9**. These numbers describe different artifacts. The author-held article **7.2.21** retains its research-source and evidence bindings and is supplied separately.

## What changed

The current source makes analytical RiskCase identity independent of formal organizational register approval. It grounds contextual EvidenceItem use through EvidenceUseRelator and a distinct reference participation role. Generalization sets now use explicit classification criteria; valid subtype links are retained. Organization-specific approval and readiness requirements remain expressible in application profiles.

The source-bound reader covers 82 catalog concepts in eleven subdomains plus one external anchor. The pharmaceutical literature provides a development context for the general domain. Earlier SQL/corpus evidence and visualizations retain their original model versions and are clearly identified. [Current visual and source routes](Visualization.md) explain which view represents which model.
