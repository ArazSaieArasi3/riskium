This is the **R2a prepared package**. It does not itself assert that a release,
maintained Wiki or Pages deployment has happened. R2b must publish the exact
verified assets, download them publicly, reconcile maintained entry points and
read them back. #92 and #93 close only after their own final publication criteria
pass. Manuscript **7.2.21** is unchanged and article binaries are not included.

The publication record must remain separate from this reproducible freeze
receipt. Existing releases and historical receipts are preserved. Rollback means
restoring the prior maintained pointers/deployment and recording the rollback;
it does not erase a published release or rewrite historical results.
