# Modeling decisions

The current source retains analytical case identity across its descriptions and administrative record states. Register membership and approval are local profile concerns. Documentary claims of responsibility are distinct from grounded responsibility relations; descriptions of vulnerabilities are distinct from their actual bearers and intrinsic conditions.

An EvidenceItem is contextual evidential participation. EvidenceUseRelator mediates it and a distinct EvidenceReferenceRole with explicit grounding, context and support/challenge/context purpose. An ordinary reference does not establish actual evidential use or make a claim true.

Generalization sets express declared common criteria. The two blanket mixed-criterion sets are removed, while all 67 subtype links are retained. The separate AgentKinds exclusivity remains; contextual roles are not made globally exclusive. The [set policy](Set-policy.md) gives the exact retained groups.
