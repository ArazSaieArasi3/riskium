# Overview

Riskium connects identifiable analytical risk items with their documentary, classificatory, assessment, treatment, responsibility, workflow, evidence, monitoring and policy information. Its eleven subdomains organize one general domain. Ecosystem and enterprise application contexts can share core meanings while declaring local requirements.

Read [Home](Home.md) for the current artifact roles, [Scope](Scope.md) for RiskCase identity and [the complete dictionary](README.md) for source-derived definitions and relationships.
