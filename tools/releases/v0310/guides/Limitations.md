# Limitations and interpretation

Riskium is a developing ontology for ecosystem and enterprise risk knowledge. The current artifact has source-bound engineering evidence for selected structural, logical, data-task and packaging behavior. It does not establish independent whole-model semantic accuracy, optimal semantic modularization or successful use across every organizational scale.

The pharmaceutical literature and derived records provide a development context. Historical candidate/admission judgments retain their own profile; they are not evidence of current operational adoption. Bounded synthetic SQLite/RDF slices do not constitute full model-to-database coverage, production performance or PostgreSQL deployment.

The documentary source review is useful context, not a universal equivalence or standards certification. Broader expert assessment, curated source alignments and application evaluations retain their separate research plans. These limits do not erase the implemented identities, projections and tested cases; they define the evidence currently available for them.
