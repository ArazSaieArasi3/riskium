# Scope and RiskCase identity

Riskium's domain is general risk knowledge, organized into eleven subdomains. Ecosystem and enterprise are intended application contexts; the pharmaceutical development material does not define the ontology's domain or establish operational validity at every scale.

A **RiskCase** is an identifiable risk item for analysis and tracking relative to objectives and context. Threat or opportunity depends on the stated perspective. Equal labels or scores do not establish identity. Several descriptions or register entries may concern one case. Documented continuity, split or merge decisions retain source references and lineage.

Formal organizational register acceptance is application-specific, not a universal existence condition. Each entry concerns one case; a case may have no entries. Context and objective constraints remain in the conceptual core. An incomplete explicit record can fail a selected data task without proving that the represented item cannot exist.

The shared core preserves meanings and identity boundaries. A declared local profile may add approval, completeness, responsibility and authorization rules while keeping core identifiers and meanings stable. Ecosystem participants can retain their distinct objectives, contexts and local policies rather than treating one organization's acceptance as universal.
