# Visualization and source inspection

Use the [current subdomain matrix](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/concepts/Subdomain-matrix.svg) and [subdomain descriptions](Subdomains.md) for the eleven subdomains and their association structure. The [association index](Relations.md), [inheritance index](Generalizations.md), [constraints](Constraints.md) and [set policy](Set-policy.md) expose distinct structural relationships rather than treating every connecting line as one kind of relation.

The current formal artifacts are the [native OntoUML exchange](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/source/riskium.ontouml.json), [OWL/Turtle](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/source/riskium.owl.ttl) and [authored catalog](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/source/riskium-model.json). The complete dictionary offers one page per concept with its definition, identity basis, direct relationships and source references.

The retained [WebVOWL](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/webvowl/index.html), [WIDOCO](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/widoco/index-en.html) and [native graphical reference](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/reference/native/index.html) are historical **model 0.3.1** views, explicitly labeled as such. They are not presented as regenerated visualizations of 0.3.2. Use the current formal files or dictionary to inspect the successor repairs.

The earlier selected evidence fragment remains a [source-bound local view](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/ce0baefa3495d1bc3b42f56ad578154d5bc4f178/evaluation/manuscript-review/v7.2.3/issue93-integration/evidence-fragment.svg), not a substitute for the complete model.
