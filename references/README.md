# Method and vocabulary references

The references below identify the method and vocabulary context of the conceptual design. They are not a claim of complete implementation, exhaustive standards coverage or regulatory compliance. A complete edition- and clause-qualified evidence corpus remains a tracked review task.

- R. de Almeida Falbo. *SABiO: Systematic Approach for Building Ontologies*. ONTO.COM/ODISE, 2014, CEUR Workshop Proceedings 1301. [Primary paper](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-1301/ontocomodise2014_2.pdf). Role: reference and operational ontology engineering activities.
- M. C. Suárez-Figueroa, A. Gómez-Pérez and B. Villazón-Terrazas. *How to Write and Use the Ontology Requirements Specification Document*. 2009. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-05151-7_16). Role: requirements specification and competency-question organization.
- [OntoUML documentation](https://ontouml.readthedocs.io/en/latest/). Role: conceptual notation and relation interpretation; the current JSON and figures are not a native-editor validation result.
- ISO 31000:2018. *Risk management — Guidelines*. [Official overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/31000). Role: selected risk-knowledge terminology and interpretation context; complete process conformance is outside the claim.
- ISO 31073:2022. *Risk management — Vocabulary*. [Committee publication page](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc262/home/projects/published/iso-310732022-risk-management--v.html). Role: selected vocabulary comparison; inspected portions and access limits must be recorded for any detailed result.
