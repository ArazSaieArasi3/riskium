## Disposition for the ten-page article

The author-held 7.2.0 citation order and ten-page body remain untouched. Its III-A treatment comparison is broadly consistent with the newly inspected ROSE portions. The IV-D sentence on “risk item” needs a small edit in the **next** article candidate: keep it expressly as local wording or omit it, with Risk IT cited only for enterprise IT-risk context. A larger ROSE/WATCHDOG/attack-tree section is not justified by the paper's available space or absence of a common benchmark. Any further source interpretation must carry its own locator and counterexample.

The result is a narrower, more defensible claim profile. **Not completed:** full ISO/Risk IT/COBIT normative crosswalks, a source-qualified Risk Item equivalence, independent ratings, and a universal historical compatibility search. They are not silently converted into passes. Independent judgments remain [#23](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/23); any later adoption of revised semantics requires model/ORSD/OWL/DB compatibility review and a separately versioned manuscript/source package. The evidence above closes only a bounded source-disposition pass, not those future studies.
