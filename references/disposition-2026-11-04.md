## Lead A

**Resolved bibliographic identity:** Ricardo Gacitua and Mauricio Diéguez-Rebolledo, “Toward a Unified View of Cybersecurity Ontologies: A Systematic Review and Conceptual Consolidation,” Applied Sciences, 16(12), article 6185, 18 June 2026. DOI: [10.3390/app16126185](https://doi.org/10.3390/app16126185). [Publisher record](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/16/12/6185).

**Actual access:** direct publisher/DOI requests returned HTTP 429. PDF/XML endpoints were inaccessible through the retrieval service. Search retrieval of the publisher's own indexed page supplied its bibliographic header, Abstract, Introduction, selected coding/synthesis passages, and Discussion. These portions were read; the complete PDF, appendices, individual 93 study records and supplementary artifacts were not inspected. The retrieved page is publisher-origin content, but this is partial indexed-text access, not a successful full-text download.

**Source finding and boundary (original paraphrase):** the authors review 93 cybersecurity ontologies and distinguish their representation, conceptual organization and reasoning support. Their synthesis discusses reusable security concepts and evaluation practice. This provides Theme 1 context and motivates cautious evidence reporting; it does not evaluate Riskium or establish absence of organizational documentary models. Its statements about a cybersecurity corpus cannot establish a universal organizational-risk gap.

**Decision:** retain this resolved source in the companion register as contextual reading. Do not add a model-comparison row or new citation to the next ten-page manuscript on this partial inspection alone. Existing direct model sources remain more specific to the article's distinctions. Reconsider a short contextual citation if a later revision uses an explicit review-level claim; that would require its own locator and citation-order check.
