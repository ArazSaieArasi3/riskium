# Requirements engineering

SABiO is the primary method for continuing ontology engineering. The Ontology Requirements Specification Document uses the ORSD guidance of Suárez-Figueroa and colleagues. UFO grounds conceptual commitments; OntoUML is the intended conceptual notation. EARS is an optional writing aid, not a substitute for competency questions or ontological analysis.

Each current requirement records its meaning, scope, rationale, relevant model elements, expected result, rejection conditions and review status. Requirements, competency questions and interpretation constraints are related but distinct records. Nonfunctional requirements need not be forced into a query.

Ontology content requirements are separate from software behavior, data admission rules and database implementation obligations. A foreign key, an OWL axiom and a SHACL shape have different semantics.

Tests distinguish representational adequacy, logical consistency, permitted and prohibited interpretations, query answers and explicit-data completeness. Open-world reasoning does not turn a missing asserted value into a closed-world validation failure.

Historical acquisition records are maintained outside this public export. The current public obligations are not a claim that every earlier requirement or competency question has been accepted or executed.
