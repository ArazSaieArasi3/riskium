# Riskium release roadmap after v0.3.9

Date: 11 November 2026. Source release v0.3.10 is publicly published and its Pages deployment succeeded; native Wiki synchronization and complete channel acceptance remain open. Subsequent package numbers are scoped proposals, not created tags or evidence of implementation, until their selections and acceptance gates are qualified. Package, ontology, database and documentation versions remain independent.

## Goal of the next release

**v0.3.10 selects a source-qualified external-definition curation component for the current 0.3.2 ontology, preserving authored meanings and historical source evidence, and making the corresponding concept comparisons consistently available through repository, Wiki and Pages.**

Do not make a full database, complete pharmaceutical reconstruction, all semantic metrics or a commercial product prerequisites for this bounded release.
