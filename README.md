# Riskium

Riskium is a general conceptual ontology for organizing **ecosystem and enterprise risk knowledge**. A **RiskCase** is an identifiable risk item for analysis and tracking relative to objectives and context; it may represent a threat or opportunity from a stated perspective. Register entries, scenarios, occurrences, assessments and evidence describe related aspects without replacing its identity. Formal organizational approval is a local application-profile condition, not a universal existence requirement.

**Published source:** [v0.3.10](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/releases/tag/v0.3.10) · **conceptual model and OWL:** 0.3.2 · **documentary review:** 0.2.0 · **Pages reader:** 0.4.10 · **explicit-data task profile:** 0.1.0. ## Current scientific source

The authoritative catalog is [ontology/0.3.2/riskium-model.json](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/ontology/0.3.2/riskium-model.json), with its [native OntoUML exchange](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/ontology/0.3.2/riskium.ontouml.json), [bounded OWL/Turtle projection](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/ontology/0.3.2/riskium.owl.ttl) and [export contract](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/ontology/0.3.2/source-contract.json). The reader covers **82 catalog concepts in eleven subdomains plus one external anchor**. These subdomains organize one general domain; pharmaceutical literature supplies a development context.

This source implements the adopted analytical RiskCase meaning, grounded contextual EvidenceItem use and explicit criterion-based generalization sets. Shared meanings and identifiers remain stable while local profiles add readiness and governance requirements. [The core/profile design decision](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/blob/main/docs/design/semantic-core-and-application-profiles.md) explains this balance.

## Historical artifacts and open research

Root model 0.3.1, its native/OWL views, historical SQL/corpus fixtures and prior receipts retain their original source roles. They do not migrate the full database or operationally admit historical withheld pharmaceutical candidates. Full mappings, SQLite/PostgreSQL implementation, dataset reconstruction, [independent semantic review #23](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/23), [semantic modules/COH/COP #77](https://github.com/ArazSaieArasi3/riskium/issues/77) and product/application evaluation remain in the [work plan](https://arazsaiearasi3.github.io/riskium/current/guide/Work-Plan.html).
